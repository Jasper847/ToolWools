About ToolWools

Our Mission

We're on a mission to build professional-grade tools that respect your privacy. Every tool we create is designed with one principle: your data belongs to you and should never leave your device.

What Makes Us Different

  • 100% Client-Side — Every tool runs entirely in your browser. Your files, text, and data are processed locally and never uploaded to any server.
  • No Accounts Required — No sign-ups, no logins, no email verification. Just open a tool and start working immediately.
  • No Data Collection — Zero cookies, zero analytics, zero tracking. We don't know who you are and we don't want to.
  • Free Forever — All tools are completely free with no usage limits, no premium tiers, and no hidden paywalls.

What We Offer

ToolWools provides 80+ professional tools across 6 categories:

  • SEO Tools — Meta tag generators, schema markup, keyword analysis, SERP tools
  • Image Tools — Compression, conversion, resizing, format optimization
  • PDF Tools — Compress, merge, split, convert PDFs
  • Text Tools — Word counter, case converter, find & replace, character tools
  • Developer Tools — JSON formatter, base64 encoder, hash generator, regex tester
  • AI Tools — Meta descriptions, keyword extraction, ad copy, hashtag generation

Built by Developers, for Developers & Marketers

We're a team of developers and marketers who were tired of tools that require accounts, upload your data to unknown servers, or hide features behind paywalls. So we built the platform we always wanted — fast, private, and genuinely useful.

Every tool is crafted to solve a real workflow problem, not to generate engagement metrics or collect your information.
