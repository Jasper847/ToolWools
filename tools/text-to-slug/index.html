SEO Slug Best Practices

  • Keep slugs under 60 characters for best SEO
  • Use hyphens (-) not underscores as separators
  • Include your primary keyword in the slug
  • Avoid stop words (the, a, an, in, is, etc.)
  • Never use special characters or spaces
