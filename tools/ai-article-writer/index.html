Frequently Asked Questions

Does this AI Article Writer require APIs or accounts?

No. This tool operates 100% client-side in your browser. It uses local template engines and semantic mapping models to build text drafts, requiring no external APIs, server calls, or account registration.

How does the writer implement copywriting frameworks?

The system evaluates your inputs against standard patterns (like AIDA: Attention, Interest, Desire, Action; and PAS: Problem, Agitate, Solution) to construct structured paragraph blocks and outlines.

Are my topics and writing drafts private?

Yes. All generation happens locally in your browser memory. No inputs are logged or sent across the network, ensuring complete confidentiality.

Can I use this content directly on my blog?

Yes! The generated outlines, introductions, and listicle points are excellent structures to kickstart your drafts. We recommend editing the generated content to align with your personal voice.

What size or count limits exist?

There are no limits! You can generate as many outlines and copywriting structures as you need offline, completely free of charge.
