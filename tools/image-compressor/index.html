FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does browser-side image compression work?

When you select an image, it is read directly into your browser using standard HTML5 APIs. We draw the image onto an HTML5 Canvas and export it using high-performance pixel scaling at your specified compression density. The compression runs entirely on your GPU and CPU locally.

Are my images sent to any server?

No. Your privacy is 100% guaranteed. All processing happens right on your device. Unlike traditional web converters, we do not upload, scan, or store your pictures. It is secure, private, and works even when you are offline!

Will I lose quality when compressing?

Our compressor uses advanced lossy algorithms for JPEG and WEBP formats, and lossless compression mapping for PNG. By optimizing the color channels and removing visual redundancy invisible to the human eye, we reduce the file size dramatically while maintaining a razor-sharp output.

Is there a limit on image dimensions?

There are no arbitrary limits set by us. However, browser hardware limitations apply (usually up to 16,384 x 16,384 pixels on modern computers). Images up to 50MB in file size can be optimized smoothly.
