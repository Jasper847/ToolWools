Frequently Asked Questions

Why do email subject lines include preheader text?

Preheader text is the preview snippet displayed in email clients (like Gmail or Outlook) next to the subject line. Optimizing both increases curiosity and drives open rates.

Are my mailing topics or audience details kept confidential?

Yes. The entire analysis runs client-side in browser RAM, ensuring zero data is transmitted over the network.

What character counts are recommended?

Email subject lines should stay under 50 characters to prevent truncation on mobile devices. The tool displays a color indicator showing length compliance.

How does the tool categorize tones?

The builder applies distinct frameworks (like **Urgency** which uses timeline boundaries and **Curiosity** which prompts open-loop thoughts) to fit target audiences.

Is there a cost to generate subject lines?

No. Like all ToolWools utilities, this email marketing assistant is 100% free with no registration requirements.
