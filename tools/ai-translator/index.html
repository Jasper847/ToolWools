Frequently Asked Questions

How does the offline translation engine operate?

This tool maps inputs against a client-side multilingual vocabulary dictionary covering common travel terms, directions, numbers, and basic sentence parameters across 10 major languages.

Is my translated data kept confidential?

Yes. The entire database mapping executes client-side. No phrases are transmitted to external servers, protecting your privacy.

What happens if a phrase is not in the dictionary?

If the exact phrase is missing, the engine tokenizes the sentence and performs a word-by-word translation, showing a literal representation to help you understand basic meanings.

Does this require an internet connection?

No. Once the page is loaded, the translation dictionary is cached in your browser, allowing you to use the tool completely offline.

Is there a limit on translation counts?

No. You can translate terms and vocabulary completely free of charge with no limitations.
