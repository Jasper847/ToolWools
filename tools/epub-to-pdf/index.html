Frequently Asked Questions

Are my copyright books sent to external servers for conversion?

No. The conversion logic runs completely local to your browser using `JSZip` to extract XML/XHTML assets and `jsPDF` to render pages. No data is transmitted online, keeping your files completely secure and private.

Does the converter support ebook images and text formatting?

Text hierarchies, paragraphs, headings, and lists are fully preserved. Dynamic stylesheet designs and nested e-book vectors may be cleaned up during the compilation layout run to export standard, printable document streams.

Is there a restriction on file size or page count?

We suggest limiting uploads to 30MB. The client-side extractor is optimized for standard epub ebooks and compiles them into separate, ordered pages in seconds.

How does the book style margins mode compare to continuous flow?

**Book Style** applies a standard page margins rule (20mm header/footer bounds) to mimic print copies. **Continuous Flow** reduces margins to fit maximum characters, minimizing total page count.

Is this converter compatible with mobile browsers?

Yes. The entire code compiles on standards-compliant modern browsers, allowing you to convert ebooks on computers, tablets, or mobile phones.
