Pixel Interpolation and Image Convolution Matrixes

Image enhancement operates by applying mathematical transformations across a grid of pixels. When adjusting contrast, saturation, or exposure, Javascript reads raw pixel values (each consisting of Red, Green, Blue, and Alpha channels) and performs mathematical operations. Exposure scales color channels exponentially, while Contrast stretches pixel values away from a neutral gray midpoint to create bolder differences between highlights and shadows.

Convolution Filtering Kernels

To sharpen or blur an image, pixel math utilizes a 3x3 convolution kernel. A kernel is a small matrix of numbers that slides across every pixel in the image. For each pixel, the kernel multiplies the target pixel and its neighbors by the corresponding matrix weights, sum-totals the values, and overrides the target color. A Sharpen Kernel uses positive center weights with negative neighbor borders to heighten adjacent edge contrasts, making details look crisper.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the sliding comparison window work?

The sliding preview places two identical canvas elements directly on top of each other. The "Before" canvas sits at the bottom, while the "After" (enhanced) canvas sits inside an overlay container. Dragging the center handle adjusts the width of the top container, showing a split before/after view.

What does the Sharpen filter actually do to the photo?

The Sharpen filter applies a 3x3 mathematical matrix to calculate contrast boundaries between adjacent pixels. It increases brightness differences along edges, making textures look crisper and restoring details in slightly blurry areas.

Will enhancing my photo reduce its resolution?

No, the canvas editor processes the original pixel dimensions of your uploaded image. Adjusting colors, brightness, or sharpness updates the values without reducing the image's height or width.

Does this tool support raw camera files (RAW)?

Browsers cannot read raw camera files directly. This tool supports standard web formats like PNG, JPEG, WebP, and SVG. To edit raw photos, convert them to PNG or JPEG before uploading.

Are enhanced photos saved or sent to any servers?

No, all photo modifications are computed locally in your browser's memory using HTML5 canvas. No files or pixel data are uploaded to the web, keeping your images completely private.
