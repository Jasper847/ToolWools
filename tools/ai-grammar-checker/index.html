Frequently Asked Questions

Does this grammar checker secure my privacy?

Yes. 100% of the spelling comparisons and syntax audit logic executes locally in your browser. None of your entered paragraphs are transmitted to external servers, protecting your privacy.

What types of grammatical issues are checked?

The checker identifies spelling mistakes (using a dictionary of common errors), double spaces, repeated punctuation marks, incorrect capitalization (such as uncapitalized words starting sentences), and lowercase 'i' pronouns.

Can I auto-fix all identified errors instantly?

Yes. The "Fix All Errors" button automatically applies the recommended spelling and spacing suggestions to your input field in one click.

Is there a word count limit for checks?

We recommend inputs under 10,000 words to ensure sub-millisecond local processing speeds.

Do you offer multiple language checking?

The current offline dictionary is optimized for standard English (US/UK). Expansion for Spanish and French dictionaries is planned in future local releases.

Technical Specifications

Understanding grammatical check types and target corrections.

Error Category Common Scenario Local Correction Applied
**Spelling Typos** `teh`, `recieve`, `seperate`, `untill` Replaces with verified dictionary spellings
**Capitalization** `i am writing. here is a start.` `I am writing. Here is a start.`
**Duplicate Spacing** Multiple spaces inside sentences Collapses double space indicators to a single space
**Punctuation Typos** Repeated comma or period tags Reduces duplicate marks down to a single symbol
