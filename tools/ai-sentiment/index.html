Frequently Asked Questions

How does the client-side sentiment analysis work?

This utility uses the AFINN-165 lexical database containing pre-evaluated emotional valence scores for thousands of English words. It parses your input text, performs tokenization, checks for negation modifiers, and aggregates the scores.

Is my text data private?

Yes. To maintain complete data privacy, all text parsing and sentence calculations execute purely inside your browser memory sandbox. Your document content is never uploaded to any remote server.

What do the sentiment scores represent?

The core score represents the aggregated sum of sentiment valence weights. Individual sentiment words carry weights from -5 (highly negative) to +5 (highly positive). A net score greater than 2 indicates a positive sentiment, while less than -2 indicates a negative sentiment.

How are negation words like "not" or "never" handled?

The algorithm employs a local valence shift heuristic. If a sentiment-carrying word is immediately preceded by a negation token, the score of that word is inverted.

Does the tool work with non-English languages?

The current version uses the English AFINN-165 lexicon. For best results, input text in English.
