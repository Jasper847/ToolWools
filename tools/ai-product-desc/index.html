Frequently Asked Questions

How does the local writer formulate product copy?

This tool evaluates your product name, features, and targeted audience against marketing copy structures (like AIDA or PAS). It compiles taglines and benefits lists offline instantly.

Is my e-commerce catalogue data secure?

Yes. The entire application runs client-side in browser RAM, ensuring zero catalogue details are transmitted online.

Can I adjust the copy's emotional appeal?

Yes. You can switch between tones like **Luxury** (which uses sophisticated, high-end adjectives), **Bold** (which emphasizes performance and direct results), and **Playful** (ideal for friendly, casual consumer products).

Are these descriptions ready for Amazon or Shopify?

Yes. The output matches standard e-commerce grid styles: a punchy headline, a value narrative paragraph, and an organized feature bullet list.

Are there daily usage limits?

No. The local generator runs completely unrestricted and free of charge.
