Frequently Asked Questions

How does the local AI Summarizer calculate highlights?

This tool runs a client-side token frequency algorithm. It cleans your text, discards common structural words (stop words), counts keyword density values, and ranks sentences based on their combined keyword weight scores.

Is my text sent to any third-party AI APIs?

No. To maintain complete data privacy, all text parsing and sentence calculations execute purely inside your browser memory sandbox. Your document content is never uploaded to any remote server.

What is the optimal input text length?

The summarizer works best with texts longer than 150 words (like blog posts, news stories, or technical guides) where statistical keyword distribution patterns become distinct.

How does the summary ratio slider affect the output?

The slider adjusts the target sentence count. For example, at 30%, the algorithm selects and returns only the top 30% highest-scoring sentences, sorted in their original narrative order.

Is there a cost or usage limit?

No. Like all ToolWools utilities, this summarizer is 100% free with no registration requirements or usage limits.
