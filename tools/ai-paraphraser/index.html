Frequently Asked Questions

How does the paraphrasing tool work offline?

This tool maps input text structures against a built-in dictionary containing thousands of synonym pairings. It evaluates nouns, verbs, and adjectives and swaps them based on your selected writing tone.

Is my data secure and private?

Yes. The paraphraser is built entirely client-side. The database matching runs locally in browser RAM, ensuring that your content never leaves your system.

Can I customize individual word choices in the output?

Yes! In our premium editor view, reworded words are highlighted. Clicking any highlighted word opens a quick interactive list where you can select alternative synonyms.

Does this help avoid plagiarism flags?

By restructuring sentences and varying word choice, this tool helps you improve readability and express ideas uniquely. However, always review output to maintain absolute semantic correctness.

Are there daily limits on how many words I can paraphrase?

No. Since all operations run locally, there are absolutely no limits, caps, or paywalls.
