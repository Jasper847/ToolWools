Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI Title Generator work offline?

This tool combines your topic with high-performing headlines templates derived from copywriting best practices. It scores character lengths locally to ensure titles fit standard Google SERP snippet widths.

What is the optimal character length for SEO titles?

Search engines generally truncate title tags after 60 characters. The tool displays a color-coded tag showing you exactly which headlines fit this limit.

Are my topics sent to external servers?

No. All parsing and outline calculations are completed client-side in browser memory. None of your inputs are sent across the web.

Can I generate titles for YouTube and social media?

Yes. The CTR Trigger and Curiosity filters are designed to optimize click rates across video search algorithms and social timelines.

Is there a cost to generate headlines?

No. Like all ToolWools utilities, this builder is 100% free with no registration requirements.
