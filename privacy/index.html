Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

ToolWools is committed to protecting your privacy. The short version: we process zero user data. All of our tools run entirely client-side in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or transmitted to any server.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No cookies of any kind (no session cookies, no tracking cookies)
  • No analytics or tracking scripts (no Google Analytics, no Hotjar, no Meta Pixel)
  • No personal data collection whatsoever
  • No account registration or login system
  • No email addresses, names, or contact details stored
  • No usage data, behavioral data, or telemetry

File Processing

When you upload files to any ToolWools tool (images, PDFs, text files, etc.), those files are processed entirely within your browser using JavaScript. Your files never leave your device. They are not uploaded to any server, not stored in any database, and not accessible to ToolWools or any third party.

Once you close or refresh the browser tab, all file data is permanently cleared from memory.

Third-Party CDN Libraries

ToolWools loads the following third-party libraries from CDNs to provide tool functionality. These CDNs may log standard HTTP request data (IP address, user agent) as part of their normal operations:

  • Google Fonts — typography (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com)
  • pdf.js — PDF rendering and processing
  • JSZip — ZIP file creation for batch downloads
  • qrcode.js — QR code generation
  • js-yaml — YAML parsing and formatting

ToolWools has no control over the privacy practices of these CDN providers. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies if you have concerns.

Local Storage

ToolWools may use your browser's localStorage to save your theme preference (light/dark mode). This data stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

Children's Privacy

Since ToolWools does not collect any personal data from any user, we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Our service is safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Since we collect no data, changes are unlikely to affect you.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@toolwools.com.
