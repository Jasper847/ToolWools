Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Our Commitment to Privacy

At ToolWools, your privacy is not just a priority — it's a fundamental design principle. We built every tool to work entirely in your browser, ensuring your data never leaves your device.

Data Collection

We collect absolutely no data. ToolWools does not collect, store, process, or transmit any personal information, usage data, or files. Period.

  • No cookies
  • No analytics or tracking scripts
  • No user accounts or sign-ups required
  • No server-side processing of your files
  • No IP address logging
  • No fingerprinting or identification techniques

How Our Tools Work

All 80+ tools on ToolWools run 100% client-side using JavaScript in your web browser. When you use any tool — whether compressing an image, formatting JSON, or generating meta tags — all processing happens locally on your device. Your files never leave your computer.

Third-Party CDN Libraries

To provide functionality, we load the following libraries from external CDNs:

  • Google Fonts — for typography (loaded from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com)
  • pdf.js — Mozilla's PDF rendering library for PDF-related tools
  • JSZip — for ZIP file handling in batch operations

These CDN providers may log standard web request data (IP addresses, user agents) according to their own privacy policies. We have no access to or control over any such data.

Local Storage

Some tools may use your browser's localStorage to save preferences (like dark mode settings). This data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

Children's Privacy

Since we collect no data whatsoever, there are no special concerns regarding children's privacy. Anyone can use ToolWools without providing any personal information.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our privacy practices, we will update this page. However, our core commitment will never change: your data stays on your device.

Contact
